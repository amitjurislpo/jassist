How JurisAssistMe supports accounting & cpa firms

Your dedicated professional follows your procedures and review steps, freeing your team for advisory and review work. JurisAssistMe provides administrative and bookkeeping support and is not a CPA firm.

Because JurisAssistMe is a managed service, you are introduced to your professional and approve them before they start, and coordination continues once they join.

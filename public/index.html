About our services

Virtual Assistant Services & Remote Teams for U.S. Businesses

J Assist provides virtual assistant services and dedicated remote teams for U.S. businesses. Whether you need a virtual executive assistant, lead generation support, bookkeeping support or a complete remote team, we match you with professionals who work as part of your business.

Unlike a freelancer marketplace, J Assist is a managed service: we match, coordinate and support your professional, and you interview them before they join. It is a practical alternative to traditional hiring for law firms, real estate agents, accounting firms, e-commerce stores and small businesses.

Explore how it works, learn why businesses choose managed virtual assistants, or contact us to build your team.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is J Assist?

J Assist provides dedicated virtual professionals and managed remote support to U.S. businesses across operations, sales, marketing, finance, real estate and technology.

Is J Assist a freelancer marketplace?

No. We match, manage and support professionals who work as part of your team, with coordination, performance monitoring and continuity handled for you.

Which time zones do you cover?

We support U.S. businesses across Eastern, Central, Mountain and Pacific time, with flexible working hours.

About our services

Virtual Assistant Services & Remote Teams for U.S. Businesses

JurisAssistMe provides virtual assistant services and dedicated remote teams for U.S. businesses. Whether you need a virtual executive assistant, administrative support, bookkeeping support or a complete remote team, we match you with professionals who work as part of your business.

Unlike a freelancer marketplace, JurisAssistMe is a managed service: we match, coordinate and support your professional, and you interview them before they join. It is a practical alternative to traditional hiring for law firms, real estate agents, accounting firms, e-commerce stores and small businesses.
